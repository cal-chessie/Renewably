# Pricing - Renewably

Renewably is an AI workforce for solar PV installers in Ireland. It is a managed monthly service, not self-serve software. No lock-in, cancel any time.

## Monthly service
- Price: EUR 1,000 to 1,500 per month, VAT exclusive
- What sets the price: team size and how many AI agents you run
- Included: the AI agents you deploy, an operations dashboard, direct support
- Billing: monthly in advance, invoiced by email, due within 14 days

## One-time setup
- A one-off setup fee covers configuration, integrations, and onboarding
- Confirmed in writing before you sign, nothing charged before that

## AI model usage
- You bring your own AI provider keys and pay model usage (for example OpenAI) directly to the provider
- Renewably does not mark up or intermediate these costs

## How you start
- A short call scopes the business, no card required
- Most installers start with the front office and add agents as they approve each one
